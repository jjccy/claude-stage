# Claude — reading

**Trigger:** tool_use: Read  
**Frames:** 6  
**ms/frame:** 280

## What it communicates
Carefully examining a file. Deliberate, focused. Not rushing.

## Movement description
Both arms are extended downward-forward, as if holding a document flat (like reading a piece of paper). They stay **still** throughout the animation — the arms are the "locked" element. What moves is the head: a 1px tilt left-right (subtle body oscillation) and the LED sweeping left-to-right-to-left in sync. A tiny scan dot appears at row 36 at the edge positions to suggest the laser reading beam touching the document.

## What makes it visually distinct from searching
- Arms point **downward** (toward a document) vs. searching's forward reach
- Arms are **still** (locked) vs. searching's motion
- Head tilt is different from searching's fixed forward lean
- Slower timing (280ms vs 200ms) — deliberate vs. frantic
- LED sweep is a reading-pace sweep, not search-scan fast
