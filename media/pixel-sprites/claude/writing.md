## Movement description
Like a pianist: left and right arms alternate up/down in anti-phase while both stay in the "forward" position (extended in front of body). Body is consistently leaned forward 2px. Every other frame has a tiny bright pixel at row 37 (keystroke flash). Arms move through a ~4-row vertical range each cycle.

## What makes it visually distinct from searching
- Arms move **vertically** (up/down) vs. searching's horizontal stretch
- Arms stay in front-and-down zone, not stretched wide
- Alternating arm phases = visible "typing" rhythm
- Keystroke flash pixel is different from searching's scan dot
