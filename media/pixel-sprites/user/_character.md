# User — Character Design

## Concept

A small hovering alien. Rounded body, big magenta eyes, a curling antenna, and stubby arms/fins — more blob-like than humanoid. Moves by floating rather than walking. Conveys "the human operator" through a friendly, slightly goofy alien aesthetic.

## Anatomy at 24×40

```
Rows  0– 2  antenna (curling, top glow)
Rows  3–11  head (large, rounded top — wide eyes take up most of the face)
Rows 12–14  neck — barely exists, blends into body
Rows 15–26  body (oval — wider than head; stubby arm-fins on sides)
Rows 27–33  lower body / hover zone
Rows 34–37  hover glow / fin area
Rows 38–39  hover shimmer (ground glow effect below body)
```

## Key design notes

- **Eyes** are the most important readable feature — large magenta ovals (3px wide, 2px tall) occupying rows 6–8. They should visibly change between states (wide open, squinting, looking up).
- **Antenna** curls at the top — not just a straight line. The curl direction can change to show mood.
- The character **hovers** — no legs. Instead, the bottom has a shimmer/glow effect. This glow should pulse in idle, intensify when moving.
- **Body is oval** — wider in the middle (rows 19–22 are the widest).
- Arms are tiny fins that stick out from the sides of the body (rows 17–20). They have limited movement.
- The user character has only 3 states (simplest character) but each should feel clearly distinct.
