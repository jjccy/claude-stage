# User — waiting

**Trigger:** permission prompt (user must respond)  
**Frames:** 6  
**ms/frame:** 700  
**Loop:** yes

## What it communicates
Alien is the one being asked something. Shrugging — "I don't know / I need to decide."

## Movement description
The "shrug" is the key pose: both arm-fins spread outward from the body. At rest (idle) they point slightly down-sideways; in waiting they extend out horizontally and slightly upward — the classic shrug gesture. The body bobs up 1px at frames 2–3 to emphasize the shrug's expressiveness. Eyes go wide (the alien version of raised eyebrows). Antenna droops to the left at rest, rises to straight at peak shrug (like a surprised eyebrow raise). Hover glow dims — the alien is spending energy on the gesture rather than hovering efficiently.

## Arm-fin spread detail
At max spread (frames 2–4), the arm-fins should extend 3px out from the body edge on each side — this is the widest the character gets. They're at rows 17–20 on each side, extending from col 3 to col 0 (left) and col 20 to col 23 (right) at peak.

## What makes it visually distinct
- **Arms spread wide** — completely different silhouette (wider) vs. idle
- **Eyes wide** — vs. idle's calm gaze
- **Body bobs up once** — not the continuous float of idle
- Hover glow dims — power-saving feeling
